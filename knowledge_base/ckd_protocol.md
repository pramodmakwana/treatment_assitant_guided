# Liffey Clinic Protocol: Chronic Kidney Disease in Adults

> Fictional protocol for a portfolio project. Not clinical guidance.

## CKD-1 Staging
Stage by eGFR (mL/min/1.73m²): G1 90 or above; G2 60 to 89; G3a 45 to 59; G3b 30 to 44; G4 15 to 29; G5 below 15. Also classify by urine ACR (albuminuria category A1, A2, A3).

## CKD-2 Monitoring frequency
Check eGFR and ACR at least once a year. Increase frequency for stage G3b or worse, for higher albuminuria, or after any change to kidney-relevant medicines.

## CKD-3 Medicines review
Review all medicines when eGFR falls, because many medicines are cleared by the kidneys.
- Avoid regular NSAID use in chronic kidney disease.
- Metformin: see T2D-4 for kidney function limits.
- SGLT2 inhibitors: start only where kidney function meets the product information criteria.

## CKD-4 Sick day guidance
During dehydrating illness (for example vomiting, diarrhoea or fever), people should be advised by their clinician to temporarily pause certain medicines, which may include metformin, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, SGLT2 inhibitors, diuretics and NSAIDs, and to restart when well.

## CKD-5 Referral
Refer to nephrology if eGFR is below 30, if eGFR falls rapidly (a sustained fall of 25% or more with a change in stage), if ACR is very high, or if hypertension is uncontrolled despite four or more medicines.
