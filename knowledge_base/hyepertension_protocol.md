# Liffey Clinic Protocol: Hypertension in Adults

> Fictional protocol for a portfolio project. Not clinical guidance.

## HTN-1 Scope
Applies to adults (18+) with primary hypertension. Does not apply to pregnancy or children; refer these to the specialist team.

## HTN-2 Blood pressure targets
- Adults under 80: clinic blood pressure below 140/90 mmHg.
- Adults aged 80 and over: clinic blood pressure below 150/90 mmHg.
- People with chronic kidney disease and significant albuminuria may need a lower target; follow specialist advice.

## HTN-3 Choice of first medicine
- People with diabetes or with chronic kidney disease and albuminuria: offer an ACE inhibitor, or an angiotensin II receptor blocker (ARB) if an ACE inhibitor is not tolerated.
- Other adults: offer an ACE inhibitor or ARB, or a calcium channel blocker, based on age, ethnicity, tolerability and other conditions.

## HTN-4 Combinations to avoid
Do not combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB.

## HTN-5 Monitoring after starting or changing an ACE inhibitor or ARB
Check serum potassium and eGFR within 1 to 2 weeks of starting or changing the medicine. Flag for clinician review if potassium is above 6.0 mmol/L or if eGFR falls by more than 25% from baseline.

## HTN-6 Pregnancy
ACE inhibitors and ARBs are contraindicated in pregnancy. Out of scope for this protocol: refer to the specialist team.
